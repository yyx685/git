Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, “Regional medullary redox profiles in rats with atlantoaxial joint disorder,” for publication as an Original Article in Physiological Research.

We examined redox-related biochemical measurements in medullary tissue containing the rostral ventrolateral medulla and adjacent regions in a rat model of atlantoaxial joint disorder. At week 4, model rats had lower superoxide dismutase activity, higher malondialdehyde-related TBA signals and higher NOX1/NOX4 protein signals than sham-operated rats. These findings add regional biochemical measurements to the characterization of this cervical joint model. We believe they will be of interest to readers studying autonomic cardiovascular regulation and redox biology.

A related Ang II/AT1-R publication drew on blood-pressure observations from the same experimental series. The relationship between that report and the present study is described in the manuscript, and the publication is cited.

This manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. Supporting data and supplementary methods accompany the submission. The relevant ethics and disclosure statements are provided in the manuscript.
