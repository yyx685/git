Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, “Regional medullary redox profiles in rats with atlantoaxial joint disorder,” as an Original Article in Physiological Research.

The study characterizes regional medullary biochemistry in a rat model previously associated with elevated blood pressure. The concurrent week-4 comparison showed lower superoxide dismutase activity and a higher thiobarbituric-acid-derived malondialdehyde-related signal in model than sham-operated animals. A separately allocated cohort was sampled at week 8 after implant removal. Nox1/Nox4-targeted RT-qPCR estimates are reported as exploratory observations, with their assay qualifications stated explicitly. Representative Western blots provide additional qualitative protein observations. The study addresses pathological physiology and regional biochemistry, within the journal’s scope.

The manuscript distinguishes the concurrent comparison from the unmatched later cohort and does not claim a specific implant-removal effect or an established NOX-mediated mechanism. Blood-pressure observations from the underlying experimental program were also used in a related Ang II/AT1-R report, which is cited and disclosed. Those shared pressure observations are not presented here as new quantitative efficacy results. The manuscript reports the actual perioperative procedures, including the omission of postoperative analgesia, and explains the relevant limitations.

This manuscript is not under consideration by another journal. Available numerical inputs, statistical results, qPCR exports, Western blot source documents and supplementary methodological information accompany the manuscript. We include an AI-use declaration that describes assistance with polishing, editing and data checking; responsibility for the article rests with the authors.

Thank you for considering this work.
