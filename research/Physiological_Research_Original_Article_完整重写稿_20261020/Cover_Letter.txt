Please consider our manuscript entitled “Regional medullary redox profiles in rats with atlantoaxial joint disorder” for publication as an Original Article in Physiological Research.

In this study, we examined redox-related biochemical changes in medullary tissue encompassing the rostral ventrolateral medulla (RVLM) and adjacent regions in a rat model of atlantoaxial joint disorder. At 4 weeks post-surgery, model rats exhibited lower superoxide dismutase activity, higher malondialdehyde-related thiobarbituric acid (TBA) signals and higher NOX1/NOX4 protein signals than sham-operated controls. These findings provide additional regional biochemical evidence to complement the characterization of this cervical joint disorder model and may be of interest to researchers studying autonomic cardiovascular regulation and redox biology.

Blood-pressure observations from the same experimental series were used in a related publication on Ang II/AT1 receptor signaling. That publication is cited, and its relationship to the present study is described in the manuscript.

This manuscript is not under consideration for publication elsewhere. Supporting data and supplementary methods accompany the submission. Relevant ethics approval and disclosure statements are provided in the manuscript.
