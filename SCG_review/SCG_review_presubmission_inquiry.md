# Pre-submission Inquiry Draft

**To:** Editor-in-Chief, *Autonomic Neuroscience: Basic and Clinical*  
**Subject:** Pre-submission inquiry — Review on the superior cervical ganglion in cerebrovascular regulation and disease

---

Dear Editor,

I am writing to ask whether *Autonomic Neuroscience: Basic and Clinical* would consider a review article provisionally entitled:

**“The superior cervical ganglion in cerebrovascular regulation and stroke: from sympathetic circuitry to pathophysiological mechanisms.”**

The superior cervical ganglion (SCG) is a principal sympathetic relay for the head, neck, and proximal cerebral circulation. Although its role in cardiac autonomic innervation has recently been reviewed, the cerebrovascular dimension has not been systematically updated. This review would fill that gap by integrating:

1. the anatomical and neurochemical basis of the SCG–cerebrovascular axis;  
2. the electrophysiological and molecular properties of SCG neurons, including ion channels, purinergic signalling, synaptic plasticity, and single-cell transcriptomic heterogeneity;  
3. the conditional role of the SCG in cerebral blood flow regulation;  
4. the contribution of SCG overactivity to ischemic stroke, subarachnoid hemorrhage-related vasospasm, and hypertension-induced vascular remodelling;  
5. the neuro–immune–endocrine functions of the SCG; and  
6. emerging SCG-targeted neuromodulation strategies, including sympathetic block, endovascular approaches, and electroacupuncture.

We plan to include two mechanism-focused figures and one summary table of experimental SCG manipulations. We believe the topic fits the journal’s emphasis on autonomic structure, physiology, pharmacology, and pathology, and would be of interest to both basic and clinically oriented readers.

We would be grateful for your advice on whether this topic would be welcome, and whether the journal would prefer a full review or a shorter mini-review. If the topic is suitable, we would also welcome any guidance on scope, focus, or authors before formal submission.
